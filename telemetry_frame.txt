TELEMETRY FRAME SPECIFICATION

Transport:
- XBee transparent UART
- UART config: 115200 baud, 8N1
- Byte stream, raw binary
- Multi-byte values: little-endian
- float values: IEEE-754 32-bit float
- Structs are packed, no padding
- Total frame size: 139 bytes

FRAME FORMAT

[SYNC BYTES][PAYLOAD]

SYNC BYTES:
0x5A 0xA5

NOTE:
The sync word is defined as 0xA55A.
Since the RP2040 is little-endian, it is transmitted as:

0x5A 0xA5
